Features

Everything you need to learn

A focused set of tools designed to make quiz creation, studying, and tracking effortless.

Quiz Builder

Create multiple choice quizzes in seconds. Add questions, set the correct answer, and publish for anyone to discover.

Progress Dashboard

Track your scores over time with clean charts. See which subjects you're strong in and where you need to review.

AI Study Assistant

Ask the AI chatbot to explain topics, clarify wrong answers, or suggest quiz sets relevant to what you're studying.

Discover & Share

Browse public quiz sets from other learners. Share yours with the community and build a collective knowledge base.

Instant Scoring

Get your results immediately after finishing a quiz. See which questions you got right and review what you missed.

Secure Accounts

Sign up with email, keep your quizzes and progress saved securely. Your data stays yours.
